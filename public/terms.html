KitRelay is an early-access production equipment and shoot-planning service operated by Arun Pratap Singh Chandel. Contact askarun2000@gmail.com for support. These terms describe how to use this release.

Your account and crew

Use a Google account you are authorised to use. Keep access to that account secure. Workspace owners choose who joins and which role each member receives. Only store or share information you have permission to use, and tell your crew before entering their personal information.

Your work stays yours

You retain your rights to the content you enter. KitRelay processes it to provide workspace storage, sharing, exports, and the optional Calendar connection described in the privacy notice. You can export a backup from Settings. Keep a backup of information important to your work.

Use the service responsibly

Do not attempt to access another crew's workspace without permission, bypass access controls, overload the service, upload unlawful content, or use the service to harm other people. Access may be limited to address abuse or service faults.

What this release provides

This release is free to use and has no paid subscription or payment collection. Cloud storage covers workspace records, not uploaded media. Each workspace supports up to 500 equipment items and 500 shoots within a 1 MB record limit. Invitations are links you share yourself; KitRelay does not send invitation emails.

Google Calendar connection is optional. You trigger sync manually, and dates flow from KitRelay to Google. Calendar changes made in Google are not imported. Provider account restrictions, outages, or revoked permissions can interrupt sign-in or sync.

Early-access limitations

Features and limits may change as the service develops. This release does not offer an uptime guarantee. Check dates, packing lists, equipment condition, and permissions before relying on them for a real production. KitRelay is a planning aid and does not inspect physical equipment or replace your crew's safety procedures.

Leaving and getting help

You can export your work, disconnect Calendar, and contact support to request account or workspace deletion. See the privacy notice for what is retained and how requests are verified. If pricing is introduced later, it will require a separate opt-in before any charge.
