KitRelay helps production crews plan equipment, pack kits, track returns, and publish shoot dates to Google Calendar. The service is operated by Arun Pratap Singh Chandel. For privacy questions, export help, or a deletion request, contact askarun2000@gmail.com from your registered account.

Information we store

  • Google sign-in: your Google account identifier, verified email address, and display name identify your account and match invitations.
  • Workspace records: workspace names, equipment details, shoots, dates, locations, crew names, notes, activity, membership roles, and invitation email addresses are stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Service operation: secure session cookies keep you signed in. Short-lived OAuth records protect sign-in. Request-rate records and operational logs help diagnose failures and prevent abuse.

Optional Google Calendar connection

Calendar access is requested separately from sign-in. KitRelay requests calendar.app.created to create secondary calendars and manage events on calendars it creates. It does not request access to read your existing personal calendars. When you choose “Sync workspace now,” shoot names, dates, locations, crew names, and status are sent to a dedicated “KitRelay productions” calendar. Sync is one way, from KitRelay to Google.

Refresh tokens are encrypted before server storage. Disconnecting Calendar removes KitRelay's stored connection and event mappings. Previously created Google events remain in your Google account. You can also revoke access in Google Account connections.

Who can access your information

Your workspace owner controls membership. Owners and editors can update shared records; viewers can read and export them. Members can see the crew's names and email addresses. Only invite people you trust with workspace data. Cloudflare hosts the app and database, and Google provides identity and Calendar services. Necessary information is processed by these providers to deliver those functions.

KitRelay does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. KitRelay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

Account and workspace records remain until deletion is requested. Session records expire after seven days; expired sign-in, invitation, session, and rate-limit records are cleaned up daily. Activity audit records are retained for up to 90 days before daily cleanup. Cloudflare may retain operational logs and recovery copies according to its service settings.

You can export workspace records from Settings, ask your owner to remove your membership, disconnect Calendar, or request account/workspace deletion by email. We verify that you control the account before handling deletion. Shared workspace ownership and records belonging to other members may need to be resolved first. Disconnecting Google alone does not delete your KitRelay account.

Browser storage

The optional signed-out demo is stored locally in your browser and is not uploaded automatically. Clearing site data removes that local copy. Session storage remembers your selected workspace. KitRelay uses necessary sign-in cookies and does not add advertising trackers.
